## 60-second interview explanation

I built a recommender for MyAnimeList that has to work for new visitors and for new shows. The first dataset I tried looked fine, but an audit showed that 97.0% of its "interaction timestamps" in a 9,819,756-row sample were really air dates or placeholders, so a temporal split on it would have been fake. I switched to a dataset with real per-user update times and joined synopses by anime ID.

I evaluated with a global time split and ranked against the full catalog of 10,720 titles, with all tuning on validation and the test set scored once. The hybrid blends collaborative and content scores per title, moving toward content as interaction history goes to zero, so a title with no history can still be ranked.

On the test set, NDCG@10 was 0.1517 versus 0.0368 for all-time popularity, a 312.6% lift. The more meaningful comparison is recent popularity at 0.0929, where the lift is 63.3%, because anime viewing follows the airing season. On validation, recency-weighted EASE reached 0.1724 NDCG@10 versus 0.0997 without recency weighting. The hybrid did not beat plain EASE overall: 0.1517 versus 0.1521. What it adds is the ability to score new titles. Among titles with no history it reached 0.1364, across 938 users, but those titles took only 0.27% of recommendation slots, so getting more of them into the top 10 without hurting accuracy is the main thing I would improve next.
